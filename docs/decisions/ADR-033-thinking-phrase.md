# ADR-033: A rotating thinking phrase, on by default, instead of reply templates

Date: Nov 4, 2026. Status: chosen by Quiel on Nov 4, 2026. Built and tested on fakes. Clips made on Nov 4, 2026.

## Context

Reply templates (ADR-020) skip the second LLM call on simple tools. Quiel asked on Nov 3 whether a template could play as a filler while the second call runs. It could, but the LLM would then say its own version of the same thing, so the reply would repeat itself. On Nov 4 Quiel chose to keep templates off and rely on the LLM. Instead, a natural thinking message should play only when the LLM is slow, and the LLM must not say it again.

The waiting phrase from ADR-009 already does most of this. It is local audio that never enters the LLM context, plays at most once per turn, and never plays once the reply has started. It was off by default, played one fixed clip, fired at 600 ms, and fired on every tool call, including quick ones like `set_timer`.

## Decision

- **On by default** in `mac.yaml` and `pi.yaml`.
- **Five short clips rotate:** "One sec.", "Just a moment.", "Hmm, let me see.", "On it.", "Working on it." The same clip never plays twice in a row. None of them promises an answer.
- **It waits 1500 ms** after end of turn, not 600 ms. A normal reply on the live stack starts in about 1 to 2 s, so the phrase now covers the slow turns, not the usual ones.
- **Only slow tools trigger it when they start:** `search`, `fetch_content`, `get_weather`, `index_documents`, `bash`, `serve`, `write_file`, `play_music`. After a quick tool the 1500 ms timer still applies.
- **The prompt tells the model not to open with a waiting phrase,** because the device already plays one. The rule that a tool is called with no words before it was already there.
- `bench/make_phrases.py` makes the clips through our `voice-speak` server (ADR-032), in the live Cartesia voice, at the output rate, metered in the ledger. About 60 characters, about $0.003. It refuses unless `ME3_LIVE=1`.
- **The clips were made on Nov 4, 2026** through `voice-speak` against Cartesia, for $0.0029: 0.88 to 1.84 s each, at -15 to -20 dBFS RMS, with about 150 ms of quiet at each end. This was the first live run of `voice-speak`. A missing clip still falls back to the 300 ms tone, and each run logs how many real clips it found.
- Reply templates stay off, and their code stays (ADR-020).

## Alternatives

- **A template as filler while the second LLM call runs:** the LLM would repeat it. Stopping that needs the template passed into the context and a prompt rule to continue from it, which is fragile for a few hundred milliseconds saved.
- **A phrase generated by TTS each time:** it costs a TTS call and its own latency, which is the wait it is meant to cover.
- **Keep the 600 ms threshold:** most live replies would get a phrase, so it would stop sounding like a pause to think.

## Consequences

- `filler_trigger` now logs which clip played. `filler_clip` logs the list and how many real clips were found.
- The latency headline is unchanged: the phrase still never counts as first audio (ADR-009).
- The 1500 ms threshold and the slow-tool list are guesses to check against live runs.

## Update, Nov 5, 2026: quick tools wait longer

The Nov 4 live run (`20261104-202417-64248a`) played the clip on every quick tool turn (`get_time`, `set_timer`, `list_files`). The tool ended about 1.04 s after end of turn, the clip started at 1.5 s, and the reply audio, ready at about 2.5 s, waited behind it: first sound out came at 3.3 to 3.7 s. The clip made those turns about 0.85 s slower.

A quick tool, one not in `slow_tools`, now restarts the waiting timer at `after_tool_ms` (2000 ms) from the tool start. On Nov 4 the reply came 1.45 to 1.64 s after a quick tool started, so it should play first; a slow reply still gets the clip. Slow tools play it at once, as before. Checked on fakes only.

Live check, Nov 5 (`20261105-195338-eb8cc9`, Mac speakers): five quick tool turns (`get_time`, `set_timer`, `list_timers`, `add_note`, `list_notes`) had no clip and first audio 1.86 to 1.92 s after end of turn, against 3.3 to 3.7 s on Nov 4. The weather turns played the clip once each.
