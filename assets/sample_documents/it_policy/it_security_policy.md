# Lakan Systems, Inc. IT and Information Security Policy

Version 2.4, effective March 1, 2026. Owner: IT Operations and Security.

Lakan Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This policy is sample material for a voice agent demo and is not a real policy.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers every employee, contractor, and intern who uses Lakan Systems devices, accounts, networks, or data. Report security incidents to security@lakansystems.example or the IT hotline at local 4444, open 24 hours.

## 2. Accounts and passwords

- Every person has one named account. Shared accounts are not allowed, except service accounts approved by Security.
- Passwords are at least 14 characters long. A passphrase of four or more random words is recommended.
- Passwords are not changed on a schedule. They are changed at once if there is any sign they leaked.
- Do not reuse a work password on any other site.
- The company password manager is required for every work password. IT installs it on day one.
- Multi-factor authentication is required on email, chat, the HR portal, the code host, and the VPN. Use the authenticator app or a hardware key. Text message codes are not allowed.
- After 10 failed sign-in attempts the account locks for 15 minutes.

## 3. Devices

- Work is done on company laptops only. Personal laptops may not hold company data.
- Laptops have full-disk encryption, the endpoint protection agent, and automatic updates turned on. Do not turn them off.
- Screens lock after 5 minutes without use. Lock the screen whenever you step away.
- Security updates must be installed within 7 days of release, and critical updates within 48 hours.
- Personal phones may access email and chat only through the managed work profile.
- Lost or stolen devices are reported to IT within 1 hour. IT wipes them remotely.
- Laptops are replaced every 4 years, or sooner if they fail.

## 4. Software

- Install software only from the company software center. Anything else needs a ticket to IT, which reviews it within 3 working days.
- Browser extensions are limited to the approved list.
- Pirated or cracked software is never allowed.
- Public AI chat tools may not be given customer data, source code, or anything marked Confidential. The approved AI assistant in the company workspace may be used with Internal and Confidential data, but never with Restricted data.

## 5. Data classification

| Class | Examples | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Website, published brochures | No limits |
| Internal | Org chart, internal wiki | Employees only |
| Confidential | Customer lists, contracts, pricing | Need to know, encrypted when sent outside |
| Restricted | Payroll, health records, passwords, keys | Named people only, never in email or chat |

Data is kept for 7 years for finance and contracts, 3 years for support tickets, and 90 days for system logs, unless the law says otherwise.

## 6. Email and chat

- Check the sender before clicking links. Report phishing with the Report button in the mail app.
- IT will never ask for your password or an MFA code.
- Do not auto-forward work email to a personal account.
- Files with Confidential data are shared through the company drive with named people, not as email attachments.

## 7. Network and remote access

- Use the company VPN on any network that is not the office network, including home Wi-Fi.
- Office guest Wi-Fi is for visitors and personal phones only.
- Do not plug unknown USB drives into company devices. USB storage is blocked by default and can be unblocked for 24 hours through a ticket.

## 8. Access requests

Access to a system is requested through the IT portal and approved by the system owner. Access is reviewed every quarter. Access for people who leave is removed on their last day by 6:00 PM, and access for people who move teams is reviewed within 5 working days.

## 9. Incidents

An incident is any loss, leak, or misuse of data, or any compromise of an account or device. Report it within 1 hour of noticing. Do not try to fix it yourself and do not delete evidence. Security gives a first response within 30 minutes during working hours and within 2 hours outside them. Personal data breaches are reported to the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours, as the Data Privacy Act requires.

## 10. Help desk

- Help desk hours: 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.
- Priority 1 (a whole team cannot work): response within 15 minutes.
- Priority 2 (one person cannot work): response within 1 hour.
- Priority 3 (a problem with a workaround): response within 1 working day.
- Requests for new equipment: delivered within 5 working days.
- Password reset: self-service in the IT portal, or call local 4444.

## 11. Breaking this policy

Breaking this policy may lead to discipline under the Employee Handbook, up to dismissal, and access may be removed while it is reviewed.
