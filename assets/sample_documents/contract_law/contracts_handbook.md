# Lakan Systems Contracts Handbook: Contract Law Basics

Version 1.3, prepared by the Legal team for sales, procurement, and project managers.

Lakan Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This handbook is sample material for a voice agent demo. It simplifies general principles of Philippine contract law under the Civil Code and is not legal advice. Ask Legal before signing anything.

## 1. What a contract is

A contract is a meeting of minds between two or more persons, where one binds themself to give something or to render some service to the other (Civil Code, Article 1305). Obligations from contracts have the force of law between the parties and must be complied with in good faith (Article 1159).

Parties may agree on any terms they like, as long as the terms are not contrary to law, morals, good customs, public order, or public policy (Article 1306). This is called autonomy of contracts.

## 2. Essential requisites

A contract needs three things to exist (Article 1318):

1. Consent of the contracting parties.
2. An object certain that is the subject matter of the contract.
3. A cause of the obligation, which is the reason each party enters into it.

If any of the three is missing, there is no contract.

## 3. Form

As a rule, contracts are binding in whatever form they were entered into, as long as the essential requisites are present (Article 1356). Some contracts must be in writing to be enforceable. Under the Statute of Frauds (Article 1403), these include an agreement that cannot be performed within one year, a sale of goods priced at PHP 500 or more, a lease of real property for longer than one year, and a sale of real property.

Lakan Systems rule: every customer and supplier contract is in writing and signed by an authorized signatory, even when the law would accept a verbal one.

## 4. Defective contracts

- Rescissible contracts are valid but may be rescinded because they cause damage, for example to creditors (Articles 1380 to 1389).
- Voidable contracts are valid until annulled, for example when consent was given through mistake, violence, intimidation, undue influence, or fraud, or when one party could not give consent (Article 1390).
- Unenforceable contracts cannot be sued on unless ratified, for example a contract covered by the Statute of Frauds that is not in writing (Article 1403).
- Void contracts have no effect at all, for example a contract whose object or cause is against the law (Article 1409). A void contract cannot be ratified.

## 5. Performance and breach

- A party who fails to perform on time after a demand is in delay (Article 1169). In reciprocal obligations, neither party is in delay until the other performs or is ready to perform.
- In reciprocal obligations, the injured party may choose between fulfillment and rescission, with damages in either case (Article 1191).
- Nobody is liable for events that could not be foreseen, or that were inevitable even if foreseen, unless the law or the contract says otherwise (Article 1174). Lakan Systems contracts list these events in a force majeure clause.
- A penalty clause takes the place of damages unless the contract says otherwise (Article 1226). A court may reduce a penalty that is unconscionable or when the obligation was partly performed (Article 1229).

## 6. Time limits for filing a case

- An action on a written contract must be brought within 10 years (Article 1144).
- An action on an oral contract must be brought within 6 years (Article 1145).

## 7. Lakan Systems standard terms

These are the company's default positions. Changes need approval from Legal.

| PHP 500,001 to PHP 5,000,000 | Chief Financial Officer |

Non-disclosure agreements on the company template may be signed by any department head without Legal review. Any change to the template needs Legal.

## 9. Contract review process

1. The business owner fills in the contract request form in the legal portal.
2. Legal replies within 3 working days for standard templates and within 10 working days for customer or supplier paper.
3. Finance checks payment terms and pricing.
4. The authorized signatory signs, by electronic signature where possible.
5. The signed copy is stored in the contract repository within 2 working days of signing.
