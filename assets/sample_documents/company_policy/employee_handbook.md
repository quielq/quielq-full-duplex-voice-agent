# Lakan Systems, Inc. Employee Handbook

Version 3.2, effective January 1, 2026. Owner: People and Culture team.

Lakan Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This handbook is sample material for a voice agent demo and is not a real policy.

## 1. About this handbook

This handbook applies to all regular, probationary, and project-based employees of Lakan Systems in the Philippines. Contractors follow their own service agreements. Where this handbook and the law differ, the law applies. Questions go to people@lakansystems.example.

## 2. Working hours and attendance

- Core hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees choose a start time between 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM and work eight hours plus a one-hour lunch break.
- Employees log time in the HR portal every day. Missing more than three time entries in a month needs a note from the manager.
- Lateness means starting after 10:00 AM without notice. Three late days in a month lead to a verbal reminder, and five lead to a written warning.
- Overtime must be approved in advance by the manager in the HR portal. Overtime is paid at 125 percent of the hourly rate on regular days and 130 percent on rest days.

## 3. Hybrid and remote work

- Teams work in the Quezon City office on Tuesdays and Thursdays, which are anchor days. The other days may be remote.
- Fully remote work for more than two weeks in a row needs approval from the department head.
- Employees working remotely must be reachable on chat during core hours and must work from a private space for customer calls.
- Working from outside the Philippines needs written approval from People and Culture and IT at least 15 working days before travel.
- The company pays a monthly internet allowance of PHP 1,500 to employees on the hybrid arrangement.

## 4. Leave

### 4.1 Vacation leave
Regular employees earn 15 days of vacation leave a year, credited at 1.25 days a month. Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year. Unused days above that are converted to cash at the end of the year. Requests for 3 or more days go in at least 10 working days ahead.

### 4.2 Sick leave
Regular employees get 12 days of sick leave a year. A medical certificate is needed for sick leave of more than 2 days in a row. Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not converted to cash.

### 4.3 Other leave
- Birthday leave: 1 day, to be used within the birthday month.
- Bereavement leave: 5 days for an immediate family member, 3 days for a grandparent or in-law.
- Maternity leave: 105 days with pay, following the Expanded Maternity Leave Law, with an option of 30 more days without pay.
- Paternity leave: 7 days with pay, plus 7 more days that the company adds on top of the law.
- Solo parent leave: 7 days a year for employees with a solo parent ID.
- Emergency leave: up to 3 days a year, approved by the manager, drawn from vacation leave if none is left.

Probationary employees earn vacation leave from day one but may only use it after their third month.

## 5. Pay and benefits

- Pay day is the 15th and the last working day of each month, by bank transfer.
- The 13th month pay is released on or before December 15.
- Health cover through an HMO starts on the first day and includes 2 dependents at no cost. More dependents may be added at the employee's cost.
- Life insurance cover equals 24 months of base pay.
- Each employee has a learning budget of PHP 25,000 a year for courses, books, and conferences, approved by the manager.
- The company matches retirement fund contributions up to 5 percent of base pay after one year of service.

## 6. Performance reviews

Reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Ratings are Exceeds, Meets, Developing, and Below. Pay increases are decided in the December review and take effect on January 1. Promotions are reviewed in both cycles. An employee rated Below gets a 60-day improvement plan with weekly check-ins.

## 7. Code of conduct

- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated.
- Gifts from customers or suppliers above PHP 3,000 in value must be declared to Compliance within 5 working days.
- Conflicts of interest, such as a side business with a customer, must be declared in writing.
- Company information is confidential unless it is already public.
- Alcohol is allowed at company events only, and never while on duty or driving for work.

## 8. Discipline

Discipline follows due process: a written notice explaining the issue, at least 5 calendar days for the employee to reply, a hearing if asked for, and a written decision. Steps are verbal reminder, written warning, final warning, suspension, and dismissal. Serious misconduct such as fraud or violence may skip steps.

## 9. Expenses and travel

- Claims are filed in the expense tool within 30 days of the expense, with an official receipt.
- Meals during approved overtime of 3 hours or more are covered up to PHP 400.
- Domestic flights are booked in economy through the travel desk at least 14 days ahead.
- The daily meal allowance on business travel is PHP 1,200 in the Philippines and USD 60 abroad.
- Hotel caps are PHP 5,000 a night in Metro Manila and PHP 4,000 a night elsewhere in the Philippines.
- Ride-hailing is allowed for client visits and for overtime that ends after 10:00 PM.

## 10. Resignation and offboarding

Employees give 30 days written notice. The last pay and the certificate of employment are released within 30 days after clearance. Company equipment is returned on or before the last day. Access to systems ends at 6:00 PM on the last day.
